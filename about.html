About SalesHub

A Salesforce product and services company, founded in 2018 in Atlanta.

Our story

From consulting partner to product company.

SalesHub began as a Salesforce consulting partner, helping companies improve how they sell, serve and operate. What we learned project after project became NPS Survey CX OS, our app on Salesforce AppExchange, which is now our focus.

Our services teams carry the same standard: QA and development pods, Salesforce delivery and government programs, led from Georgia and delivered with our client innovation center in Dhaka.

How we work
  • Transparent with clients and with each other
  • One owner for every commitment
  • Long-term teams: our engineers often stay with a client for years
  • We invest in our people, through training and fair recognition
