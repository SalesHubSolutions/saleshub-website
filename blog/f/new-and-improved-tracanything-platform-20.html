New and Improved TracAnything Platform 2.0

June 18, 2025

Empowering Business Success with Modular Solutions The TracAnything platform is a powerful suite of Salesforce-native applications designed to streamline business processes, improve decision-making, and enhance operational efficiency. Whether you need vendor management, financial planning, project oversight, or business automation, TracSuite offers scalable, modular solutions tailored to your organization’s needs. With dedicated modules for key business functions, TracAnything offers tailored solutions to meet your organization’s unique needs.

MissionOps - The Home of TracAnything

MissionOps is your command center — a unified workspace where all TracAnything modules connect. It’s designed to bring order and visibility to your operations, so you’re never left guessing. MissionOps allows you to manage business-wide functions with full control and clarity, from project expense tracking and vendor contracts to forecasting and system integration. A unified workspace for managing business operations efficiently.

Project Expense Management: You can track and manage all project-related costs in a centralized dashboard. It eliminates guesswork and manual reconciliations, ensuring expenses are tied to deliverables and budgets in real-time.

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A): Transform financial strategy with intelligent planning tools. Built-in forecasting and scenario tools help finance teams turn insights into action. You can build budgets, adjust forecasts dynamically, and model future risks to keep spending on track.

Vendor Management System (VMS): Centralize all vendor-related activities, including onboarding, contract tracking, and performance monitoring. This streamlines compliance and strengthens third-party relationships.

Business System Analysis (BSA): Provides a complete toolkit for mapping, evaluating, and improving your business processes. It helps IT and business teams stay aligned as systems evolve.

Backoffice Business Operations (BizOps): Handles time-consuming operational processes like travel approvals, asset management, and leave requests. This automation reduces administrative overhead and increases transparency.

Track DevOps: Brings discipline to your software delivery pipeline by connecting product backlog, CI/CD workflows, and testing, all inside Salesforce.

Vendor Management System (VMS)

Optimize vendor performance, compliance, and relationships.

Managing vendors across departments often leads to fragmented communication, contract delays, and compliance risks. TracAnything VMS centralizes every aspect of vendor engagement.

Optimize vendor relationships and ensure compliance.

  • Vendor Onboarding: Automate vendor registration and approval processes using flows. This ensures that all required documentation is collected and compliance standards are enforced from day one.
  • Contract Lifecycle Management: Track every stage of a contract’s life, including initiation, renewal, expiration, and SLA monitoring. Automated reminders prevent lapses and miss deadlines.
  • Performance Tracking: Capture key performance indicators such as delivery timeliness, quality scores, and SLA compliance to ensure vendors meet expectations.
  • Secure Document Storage and Management: Upload and manage vendor-related documents securely within Salesforce. Role-based access ensures data security.
  • Alerts & Notifications: Stay on top of contract expirations, vendor scorecard reviews, and evaluation cycles with automated alerts.

The result? With VMS, procurement becomes predictable, partnerships strengthen, and compliance is enforced effortlessly.

Business System Analysis (BSA)

Drive smarter system planning and business process transformation.

Business Analysts and IT teams start by capturing and visualizing business processes using TracAnything’s BSA module. Stakeholders define requirements, map out workflows, and document system integration needs. During this phase, the BSA module becomes the foundation — it connects to PMO for project initiation and ties to DevOps for implementation alignment. Each requirement is tracked with full traceability from initial intake to delivery.

As systems evolve, gap analyses and impact assessments guide change management decisions. This structure ensures every business need is properly scoped and no integration or downstream impact is missed. Business process modeling directly feeds into sprint planning in the DevOps module, providing a direct line from requirement to release.

  • Process Mapping: Create visual workflows to identify redundancies, delays, or inefficiencies in current operations.
  • Requirements: Collect and manage stakeholder requirements directly within Salesforce, ensuring traceability from need to delivery.
  • Workflow Optimization: Automate and refine processes for improved efficiency.
  • Gap Analysis: Identify process or functionality gaps within existing systems, helping you prioritize system improvements.
  • System Integration Mapping: Visualize how different systems interact and define how data should flow between them.
  • Impact Analysis: Model and communicate the downstream effects of changes before implementation.

It’s built for Business Analysts, Architects, and IT leaders looking to design smarter systems and communicate them clearly.

Project Management Office (PMO)

Deliver projects with confidence and control.

PMO is more than task tracking, it’s a complete toolkit to drive successful delivery across teams and departments. Plan, execute, and monitor projects with precision.

  • Project Planning: Develop detailed project plans including timelines, dependencies, and stakeholder responsibilities.
  • Task & Milestone Management: Assign, monitor, and complete tasks using structured milestone tracking to maintain momentum.
  • Resource Allocation: Allocate personnel and assets effectively across projects to maximize productivity without burnout.
  • Risk Management: Identify and log potential risks. Create mitigation strategies that are tracked and monitored through project phases.
  • Progress Tracking: Visual dashboards provide insights into budget burn, completion rates, and timeline adherence in real time.
  • Document Management: Store all project-related documentation, including meeting notes, deliverables, and approvals in a centralized location.

Whether you're managing one project or dozens, TracAnything PMO keeps your teams aligned and accountable.

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)

Transforms how your organization budgets, forecasts, and makes financial decisions.

Finance leaders need tools that do more than just report numbers; they need to model outcomes and adjust in real time. FP&A in TracAnything delivers just that. Transform financial strategy with intelligent planning tools.

  • Budgeting & Forecasting: Create department-level or project-specific budgets and forecasts based on historical and current data.
  • Variance Analysis: Spot discrepancies between planned and actuals early and take action to stay on course.
  • Financial Reporting: Gain insights with dynamic dashboards and reports.
  • Scenario Planning: Model financial risks and contingency strategies.
  • Expense Tracking: Categorize and monitor expenses effectively.
  • Resource Utilization: Maximize ROI by tracking staffing and equipment.

FP&A puts finance in the driver’s seat, aligning strategy with data-driven execution.

Backoffice Business Operations (BizOps)

Streamline admin-heavy processes and eliminate manual tracking.

Every company has operational tasks that drain time, such as travel approvals, expense reporting, asset tracking. BizOps helps automate them all.

Simplify daily business functions and improve efficiency.

  • iTravel Management: Manage employee travel approvals and expenses.
  • HR and Leave Management: Automate leave requests and balance tracking.
  • Mileage Tracking: Streamline mileage logs for reimbursements.
  • Expense Management: Track, categorize, and approve expenses.
  • Receipts Management: Capture and organize receipts for audits.
  • Asset Management: Oversee IT equipment, supplies, and resources.

Track DevOps

Connect development to delivery with structure and visibility.

Software delivery doesn’t have to be chaotic. Track DevOps offers a Salesforce-native alternative to disconnected DevOps tools. Enhance software delivery with structured DevOps workflows.

  • CI/CD Pipeline Management: Automate build, test, and deployment workflows.
  • Product Backlog: Organize and prioritize development tasks.
  • Sprint & Release Scheduling: Plan sprints and ensure timely releases.
  • Issue Log: Manage development issues for better accountability.
  • UAT & System Testing: Documenting and managing testing processes.
  • DevOps Dashboard: Visualize development progress and results.

Built for teams running agile, hybrid, or waterfall methods, Track DevOps closes the gap between IT and business.

Empowering Business Success with Modular Solutions

TracAnything is a Salesforce-native enterprise platform designed to unify project execution, financial oversight, vendor management, DevOps, and daily business operations across the entire organization.

Each module is fully integrated and role aware. Every action—from a BA submitting requirements to a developer closing a user story, or a finance analyst reconciling expenses—triggers connected workflows and real-time data updates across the suite. The result is full visibility for CIOs, CFOs, and business leaders—delivered in a single system of record.

Business Process Coverage by Phase:

  • Initiation & Planning: Define goals, assign roles, allocate budget
  • Execution: Track tasks, hours, resources, and vendor delivery
  • Monitoring & Control: Automate reporting, budget burn, KPI tracking
  • Closure & Analysis: Perform retrospectives, finalize documentation, update forecasts

Data Flow Across Modules:

  • Business requirements flow from BSA → form product backlog in DevOps
  • Tasks in PMO track against deliverables, sprints, and milestones
  • Resource hours flow into FP&A for forecasting and CapEx/OpEx allocation
  • Vendor activity logged in VMS syncs with contracts, payments, and performance
  • Internal approvals (travel, leave, assets) flow through BizOps
  • All data rolls into dashboards accessed in MissionOps by project, department, or executive role
