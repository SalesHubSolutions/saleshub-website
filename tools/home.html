Client story

A finance and insurance client, release after release

A US insurance and finance company has trusted our Dhaka QA team and Salesforce engineers with its core claims and dealer systems for multiple years.

F&I client · Atlanta, GA
The challenge

Complex claims, cancellation and dealer portal releases across several brands and regions, with long manual test cycles and hard-to-debug API integrations.

What our pod did

Took over regression and new-feature testing, introduced AI-assisted automation with Katalon, automated the dealer portal end to end (pricing, cart, checkout, login, registration) and built a backup system so leave never paused testing.

What we built

Our Salesforce team delivered an AI-powered claim adjudication and management system, then a claim payment app connected to the client's AP platform, plus cancellation and reinstatement.
