FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

SalesHub Launches NPS Survey v4.4 on Salesforce – Empowering Organizations to Capture Feedback, Drive Action, and Enhance Customer Experience

Marietta, GA – August 26, 2025 – SalesHub, a leading provider of Salesforce-native solutions, today announced the release of NPS Survey v4.4, the latest version of its powerful customer feedback platform. The release delivers advanced survey customization, smarter automation, and real-time analytics, enabling organizations to improve customer experience and make data-driven decisions—all within Salesforce.

Key Features of NPS Survey v4.4:

  • Dynamic Survey Builder: Create NPS, CSAT, CES, and custom surveys with 15+ question types, branding options, multi-lingual support, and device-responsive layouts.
  • Smarter Automation & Workflows: Integrate with Salesforce Flow to auto-trigger follow-ups, case creation, escalations, and automated notifications for detractors.
  • Advanced Reporting & Dashboards: Executive and drill-down dashboards, trend analysis, sentiment insights, and export options for extended analysis.
  • Closing the Feedback Loop: Automated workflows ensure timely follow-up on low scores, task assignments, and resolution tracking.
  • Enterprise-Ready Security: Role-based access, audit logging, multi-org support, and GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA compliance.

Role-Based Benefits:

  • Admins: Simplify survey setup, enforce governance, and scale surveys across business units.
  • Architects: Seamlessly integrate surveys, workflows, and dashboards into Salesforce.
  • Business Unit Owners / Managers: Track customer feedback, close the loop faster, and drive measurable CX improvements.
  • Executives: Access real-time insights to make strategic, data-driven decisions.

Business Impact: NPS Survey v4.4 helps organizations increase survey response rates, resolve customer issues faster, and empower leaders with actionable insights to strengthen customer loyalty and satisfaction.

Availability: All existing customers will automatically transition to v4.4 starting August 30, 2025. New users can sign up or install directly from Salesforce AppExchange: https://appexchange.salesforce.com/appxListingDetail?listingId=a0N3A00000FtRTKUA3

About SalesHub: SalesHub is a trusted Salesforce ISV and cloud consulting company delivering innovative solutions to help organizations optimize customer experience, enhance operational efficiency, and unlock actionable insights.
