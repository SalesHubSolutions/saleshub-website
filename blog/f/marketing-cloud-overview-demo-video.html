Marketing Cloud: Overview Demo Video

December 4, 2020

This post is from our archive. Some product names, offers and details may have changed since it was published.

Connect with all your customers - how they want - with Marketing Cloud. Salesforce for Marketing is built on the world’s #1 CRM platform. Salesforce has an array of solutions to help you know, personalize, and engage - which give you the critical capabilities to run sophisticated marketing programs and achieve your core goals. And Salesforce has built all of this -- the ability to know, personalize, and engage - into Salesforce for Marketing solution which is build on the World’s #1 CRM platform.

Watch the video

Starting with knowing your customer, Salesforce for Marketing empowers you with...

Data integration, management, and sharing capabilities with Audience Studio and Data Studio where you can capture and manage all the online data -- so 1st, 2nd, and 3rd party data -- from any source and device to reconcile identity, segment, and activate to reach people wherever they are across the open web.

Then with Datorama, which is focused on marketing intelligence, you’re able to bring all the different marketing sources together into a unified view to visualize and measure performance and gain actionable insights on that performance, which allows you to further improve the ROI of your overall marketing.

Through Salesforce's partnership with Google and integration between Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Google Analytics 360, you’re able to use web analytics to better understand the customer journey from the web across to all the different marketing channels. This allows you to understand customer insights in one view, and then take action on those insights by engaging on key marketing channels like email, mobile, advertising, and social.

When it comes to personalizing with intelligence...

Journey Builder is the marketing orchestration engine that allows you to coordinate all marketing channels -- across email, mobile, advertising, and the web -- seamlessly with each other, as well as coordinate those channels with channels that go beyond marketing like Sales, Service, and Commerce. Salesforce also offers real-time interaction management through Interaction Studio which allows you to capture all the real-time signals across any device, visualize the customer lifecycle, and take action in real-time with the next best offer or action.

For lead management, Pardot, B2B marketing automation solution, aligns marketing (such as Advertising and Social) and sales to generate the best leads, keep them engaged, and connect with them at the right time to close more deals.

And finally, for engaging customers across all touchpoints in the journey….

You can leverage pre-built integrations to trigger actions between Marketing Cloud, Commerce, Sales, and Service -And use Salesforce core channel Studios, which are the best of breed for engaging in each key marketing channel… -With Email and Mobile Studio, Salesforce is able to do best in class messaging with AI-driven content on channels with the highest ROI including SMS, push, and chat app messages (EX: in this midterm election I got texts from local candidates).

Advertising Studio allows us to use Salesforce CRM data to securely power 1-to-1 advertising across Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, and open web display channels at scale Social Studio enables us to listen, publish, and engage to create customer advocates, and connect social to Marketing, Sales, and Service in one platform powered by AI (EX: what people are saying about issues, how propositions are trending, the sentiment of candidates, etc.).
