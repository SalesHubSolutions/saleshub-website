Release Note for NPS Survey Solution by SalesHub on Salesforce Platform

Release Date: July 2024

SalesHub is thrilled to announce the latest updates and features for our NPS Survey Solution on the Salesforce Platform. These enhancements are designed to streamline your customer feedback process, providing deeper insights and more efficient workflows.

New Features and Enhancements

1. Survey Program

  • Program Definition: Define the start and end of each survey program and set criteria for inclusion.
  • Member Management: Add contacts or leads manually or automatically based on predefined criteria.
  • Email Integration: Use List Email capabilities to send surveys with templates for NPS, CSAT, and Implementation Follow-Up.

2. Survey Schedule

  • Schedule Definition: Record and manage survey schedules, specifying manual or trigger-based execution conditions.

3. Survey Score

  • Comprehensive Scoring: Capture NPS and CSAT scores to analyze customer sentiments and trends.
  • Actionable Insights: Define actions based on customer categories (detractor, passive, promoter).

4. Survey Questions

  • Question Management: Edit, delete, or add survey questions for each program to tailor feedback collection.

5. NPS Action Plan

  • Automated Action Plans: Generate follow-up actions automatically based on NPS scores to address customer issues promptly.

6. Survey Distribution from Salesforce Objects

  • Accounts: Send surveys manually or automatically based on events like customer creation date (30, 60, and 90 days).
  • Contacts: Distribute NPS, CSAT, and Implementation Follow-Up surveys to contacts.
  • Opportunities: Send targeted surveys to gather feedback on opportunities.
  • Cases: Collect feedback on case resolutions through automated survey triggers.

Bug Fixes and Performance Improvements

  • Resolved issues with survey loading times.
  • Improved stability and performance for large-scale survey distributions.
  • Fixed minor UI/UX bugs to enhance overall user experience.

Getting Started

To start leveraging these new features, update your NPS Survey Solution via the Salesforce AppExchange. For detailed instructions, visit NPS Survey (saleshub.info)
