Meet Allison O'Neill, the driving force behind the successful launch of our cutting-edge Salesforce AI-powered Claim Adjudication and Management System for our esteemed Finance & Insurance (F&I) client.

Allison's integral role in developing the application, from ideation to implementation through MVP cycles, has been truly remarkable.
