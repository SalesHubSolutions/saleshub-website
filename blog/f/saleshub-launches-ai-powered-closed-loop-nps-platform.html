SalesHub Launches AI-Powered Closed-Loop NPS Platform Native to Salesforce AppExchange

Marietta, GA | March 27, 2026 | FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

SalesHub, LLC today announced the general availability of its next-generation NPS Survey CX Platform on the Salesforce AppExchange - - the only 100% native customer experience solution that automatically closes the feedback loop without a single integration, data export, or manual hand-off.

Built entirely on the Salesforce platform, the NPS Survey solution by SalesHub eliminates the disconnect between customer feedback and operational response. When a detractor submits a survey, a P1 case is created automatically. When a promoter responds, a review campaign fires instantly. When a passive goes quiet, a 30-day nurture journey begins. All of it happens inside Salesforce - with full audit trails, GDPR/CCPA compliance controls, and zero third-party data transfer.

The Problem It Solves

Enterprise customer success teams have long faced a paradox: they invest in NPS programs but continue to see churn. The reason is structural. Survey tools like Qualtrics and Medallia sit outside Salesforce, requiring costly integrations, manual exports, and delayed responses. By the time a detractor is identified, and a recovery action is taken, the customer has already decided to leave.

"Feedback that doesn't trigger action isn't a CX program - it's a spreadsheet exercise. SalesHub closes that gap entirely."

What's New in NPS Survey 4.4

• Agentic Loop Engine: Listen → Classify → Orchestrate → Verify - all automated

• Survey Builder Pro with 15+ question types, conditional logic, and skip patterns

• CX360 Dashboard with real-time NPS, CSAT, and CES scoring by rep, team, region

• Closed-Loop Orchestration: auto P1 cases for detractors, review campaigns for promoters

• Native Salesforce Flow integration - trigger surveys from any object, any event

• Org-based pricing starting at $149/month - 80% less than Qualtrics

• One-day deployment. No credit card required for 14-day trial

Availability & Pricing

NPS Survey by SalesHub is available today on the Salesforce AppExchange. Org-based pricing begins at $291/month for up to 10 users (NPS Essentials). Enterprise tiers with multi-org support, data residency controls, and custom SLAs start at $16,000/year. A free 14-day trial requires no credit card.
