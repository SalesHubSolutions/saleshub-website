SalesHub's Dhaka team revolutionizes testing with Katalon Studio

April 30, 2024

This post is from our archive. Some product names, offers and details may have changed since it was published.

In Q1 2024, SalesHub's Dhaka-based QA team tackled diverse projects, managing defects and deployments efficiently. They covered infrastructure migration, requirement modifications, program introductions, and security upgrades. Also, they supported multiple automotive brands across regions, ensuring consistent quality assurance.

Streamlining Testing Processes with Studio:

In the bustling city of Dhaka, Bangladesh, SalesHub's offshore QA team has been making waves in the software testing industry. Servicing a prominent insurance client based in Atlanta, Georgia, this team has exemplified excellence in their approach to testing, thanks to the implementation of cutting-edge tools and methodologies.

Challenges and Solutions:

Testing is undeniably hard work, especially when dealing with complex software systems across various platforms. The offshore QA team encountered challenges such as time-consuming testing processes, complex debugging, and API integration complexity. To address these challenges, the team adopted Katalon Studio - Write Better Tests Faster, a comprehensive testing solution designed to simplify and streamline the testing process.

Results Achieved:

The implementation of Katalon Studio yielded remarkable results for SalesHub's offshore QA team:

  • Increased Productivity: Testers were able to accomplish more in less time, thanks to Studio's intuitive interface and AI-powered automation features.
  • Improved Quality Assurance: By identifying and resolving issues more efficiently, the team could ensure higher quality software deliverables for their client.
  • Enhanced Collaboration: Studio's centralized platform improved collaboration within the team, enabling smoother communication and knowledge sharing.
  • Cost Savings: The streamlined testing process resulted in cost savings for the client, as fewer resources were required to achieve testing goals.

Automating Dealer Portals:

The team's responsibilities encompassed handling dealer development and solution testing automation, covering areas such as landing pages, plan pricing, cart functionality, checkout processes, user login, and new user registration. This automation effort significantly enhanced efficiency and reliability in the dealer portal processes.

Advancements in Claim Cancellation Processes:

The Claim Cancellation Team demonstrated remarkable collaboration and advancement in testing and validating cancellation processes. Despite varying defect/task counts, the team remained dedicated to achieving successful project deployments and continually enhancing cancellation functionalities for clients.

New Implementations Accomplishments:

The team achieved significant milestones in implementing new features and enhancements for consumer-facing teams, including functional assertions, screenshot capturing for debugging, UI rates calculation validation, and more. These implementations streamlined processes and improved overall performance and reliability.

Through innovation, dedication, and collaboration, SalesHub's offshore QA team in Dhaka, Bangladesh, has elevated QA excellence to new heights. Their success story serves as a testament to the power of cutting-edge tools, strategic thinking, and teamwork in driving efficiency and quality in software testing. As they continue to embrace innovation, they stand ready to meet the evolving challenges of the software testing landscape with confidence and expertise.
