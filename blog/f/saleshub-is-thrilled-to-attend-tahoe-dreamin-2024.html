SalesHub is Thrilled to Attend Tahoe Dreamin 2024!

July 4, 2024

This post is from our archive. Some product names, offers and details may have changed since it was published.

We are excited to announce that SalesHub will be attending Tahoe Dreamin 2024, a premier event for Salesforce enthusiasts and professionals. Join us from July 25th-27th at Harrah’s Lake Tahoe to explore the latest in Salesforce innovations and connect with industry leaders.

Highlights at Tahoe Dreamin:

  1. TracAnything - Project Management and Enterprise Planning on Salesforce - Discover how TracAnything by SalesHub can revolutionize your PMO, Operations, and Finance organizations with seamless integration with Salesforce. Enhance operational efficiency with this all-in-one project management, expense tracking, and enterprise planning solution. TracAnything transforms the way your company manages, governs, and justifies project spending. Salesforce AppExchange Link - TracAnything (salesforce.com)
  2. NPS Survey - Measure NPS, Transform Customer Service - Discover how the NPS Survey by SalesHub can transform your customer service by automatically triggering surveys based on customer activity and seamlessly integrating responses into Salesforce, enabling your team to close the loop effectively. Salesforce AppExchange Link - NPS Survey (salesforce.com)
  3. Salesforce Services for Front Office - Explore our specialized Salesforce services for the Financial & Insurance (F&I) industries, designed to streamline claim operations, enhance payment processing, and boost customer engagement in the financial sector. Learn more about our Salesforce professional services. Salesforce Services (saleshub.info)

Why Visit SalesHub at Tahoe Dreamin?

  • Hybrid PMO & ERP Solutions - Learn about our comprehensive Project Management and Enterprise Planning suite on Salesforce, designed to enhance customer, employee, and partner experiences.
  • NPS, CSAT, and Feedback Management Platform - See how we streamline processes by integrating NPS, CSAT Survey, and form data directly into Salesforce, ensuring accuracy and saving time.
  • · Connected Front Office Service for F&I Industry - Hear from Financial & Insurance (F&I) industry leaders about how SalesHub has revolutionized their claim management and payment processing mechanisms.
  • · Salesforce Center of Competencies - Discover how SalesHub solutions and services combine with Front Office-based COC using CRM datasets to uncover deep insights and drive actionable strategies.
