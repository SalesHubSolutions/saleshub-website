What is Salesforce

October 3, 2022

This post is from our archive. Some product names, offers and details may have changed since it was published.

Salesforce is a customer relationship management (CRM) solution that brings companies and customers together. Used by over 150.000 companies and nonprofits, one integrated CRM platform that gives all your departments — including marketing, sales, commerce, and service — a single, shared view of every customer. - Salesforce.com

Why should you learn Salesforce now?

Salesforce is the world's leading customer platform with more than 150.000 business customers. From 2019 to 2024, worldwide spending on cloud computing is estimated to grow 19% YoY. Salesforce is the market leader in various segments such as CRM, Customer Service, Customer Experience, Digital Marketing, and Business Analytics. Over the next 5 years, 492,900 direct jobs and 958,100 indirect jobs will be created in Western Europe. During this period, Salesforce and its ecosystem are expected to enable the creation of 4.2 million jobs globally. - IDC Report
