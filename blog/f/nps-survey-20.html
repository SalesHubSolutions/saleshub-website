NPS Survey 2.0

July 6, 2025

SalesHub Launches NPS Survey 2.0 to Help Enterprises Automate Customer Feedback and Close the Loop Atlanta, GA — July 6, 2025 Salesforce-Powered Customer Experience

When faced with the challenge of improving customer engagement and closing the feedback loop in real time, many enterprise teams struggle with manual survey tools, disconnected systems, and delayed insights. To solve this, SalesHub has launched NPS Survey 2.0, a fully Salesforce-native solution that automates survey delivery, captures responses instantly, and drives action across the customer lifecycle.

NPS Survey 2.0 is designed for teams that want to monitor customer sentiment, reduce churn, and improve service delivery. The app enables organizations to send customized surveys triggered by any Salesforce event—support case closure, onboarding completion, purchase milestone, or renewal activity—without requiring third-party tools or coding.

Built natively on the Salesforce platform, NPS Survey 2.0 integrates directly into existing CRM workflows. Survey responses are tied to Contacts, Accounts, or Cases, allowing teams to view and act on feedback in context. Follow-up tasks can be auto-assigned, escalated, or included in reports to leadership, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks.

SalesHub serves a broad range of clients across financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, and B2B SaaS. These organizations rely on timely customer feedback to guide product development, improve service quality, and strengthen customer relationships. With NPS Survey 2.0, teams can now automate what used to be a manual, siloed process.

SalesHub delivers the app with a customizable LWC-based dashboard for real-time visualization of scores, trends, and feedback by region, team, or product line. This gives managers and executives an at-a-glance view of Net Promoter Scores (NPS), customer sentiment breakdowns, and trends over time, without needing additional BI tools.

NPS Survey 2.0 is already in use across multiple U.S. enterprise environments and is helping clients improve response rates, reduce negative feedback, and strengthen retention strategies. It’s a key part of SalesHub’s broader commitment to building scalable, native Salesforce solutions that simplify operations and improve decision-making.

The app is now available via SalesHub’s implementation team and will soon be listed on the Salesforce AppExchange. SalesHub continues to invest in tools that enhance customer experience and operational excellence for Salesforce-driven organizations.
