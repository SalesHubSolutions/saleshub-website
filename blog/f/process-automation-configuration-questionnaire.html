Process Automation Configuration Questionnaire

November 25, 2020

This post is from our archive. Some product names, offers and details may have changed since it was published.

1. What are the goals and objectives of the process?

2. What roles are involved in the process? Briefly describe the duties for each role.

3. What Salesforce workflow capabilities are in place today as part of this process?

4. Where do delays exist and where do handoffs take place (between users or systems)?

5. What pain does the current process cause? Is this pain measurable?

6. What measures are used to manage the process today (i.e. KPIs)?

7. How do you intend to measure success for this process going forward?

8. What is the service level agreement for the process (formal or informal)?
