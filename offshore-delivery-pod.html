What you get

A team that feels like part of yours.

Dedicated engineers

QA, Salesforce and full-stack engineers assigned only to you, trained on your product and ramped up with a documented onboarding checklist.

A named account manager

A Dhaka-based key account manager runs the team day to day: daily check-ins, time and leave tracking, and the first call for any concern.

U.S. delivery executive

A U.S.-based executive meets your stakeholders monthly and prepares quarterly business reviews for IT leadership.

Commercial model

One fixed monthly rate.

You pay one predictable fee for the pod, not hourly invoices. The pod is sized to your release plan, and we add backup engineers when someone is out so delivery does not stop.

  • Engineers sized to your workload, from a small team to a full pod
  • Offshore key account manager and U.S. delivery executive included
  • Daily standups in your scrum, with Slack, Teams and Jira access
  • Secure access through your VDI or Windows 365
  • Transparent hours and outcomes: a tracked defect list backed by tickets
  • Weekly status report (PowerPoint and PDF) and quarterly business reviews
How we deliver quality

Testing that finds problems early.

Test automation

AI-assisted automation suites shorten test cycles, widen coverage and catch regressions before release.

Shift-left QA

QA joins at requirements, so issues are found before they reach development, let alone production.

Root cause analysis

We don't just log defects. We trace each one to its source so the same problem does not come back.

Regression runs

Regular regression runs make sure new changes don't break what already works.

Peer review

Code and test scripts are peer reviewed and checked against agreed standards.

Client feedback

We ask your engineers, managers and leaders how we are doing every quarter, and act on what we hear.
