As they delved deeper into their quest, SalesHub uncovered the magic of skill-based routing. They discovered the wonders of Email-to-Case, a feature that effortlessly transformed customer emails into actionable cases within the Salesforce Service Cloud. Here are some of the key features we include as part of our standard deployment.

1. Salesforce Service Cloud Basics:

Salesforce Service Cloud is a customer service platform that allows organizations to manage customer interactions across multiple channels. It provides tools for case management, knowledge management, and customer communication, enabling businesses to deliver efficient and personalized support to their customers.

2. Skill-Based Routing:

Skill-based routing is a feature of Salesforce Service Cloud that automatically assigns incoming cases to agents based on their skills, expertise, and availability. It ensures that each case is routed to the most qualified agent, leading to faster resolution times and improved customer satisfaction.

3. Email-to-Case:

Email-to-Case is a functionality in Salesforce Service Cloud that automatically converts customer emails into support cases. It allows businesses to manage customer inquiries and issues received via email within the Service Cloud platform, streamlining the support process and reducing manual effort.

4. Intelligent Case and Service Automation:

Intelligent case and service automation leverage artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) to automate repetitive tasks and processes within Salesforce Service Cloud. This includes tasks such as case routing, prioritization, and resolution, as well as proactive customer communication and issue resolution.

5. Entitlement:

Entitlement management in Salesforce Service Cloud defines the level of support or service that a customer is entitled to based on factors such as their service contract, product warranty, or subscription tier. It ensures that customers receive the appropriate level of support based on their entitlements, helping businesses meet service level agreements (SLAs) and deliver consistent service experiences.

6. Customer Self-Service using Salesforce Community: Salesforce Community allows businesses to create branded online communities where customers can find answers to their questions, access knowledge articles, and collaborate with peers. It empowers customers to resolve issues on their own, reducing the need for agent intervention and improving overall customer satisfaction.

7. SalesHub's NPS Solution:

SalesHub's NPS (Net Promoter Score) solution is a tool available on the Salesforce App Exchange that allows businesses to measure customer loyalty and satisfaction. It enables organizations to gather feedback from customers through NPS surveys, analyze the results, and take action to improve the customer experience.

By leveraging SalesHub's NPS solution, businesses can identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty. NPS Survey (salesforce.com).
