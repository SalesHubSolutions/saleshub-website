It’s about transformation, not translation. When we take the things we are used to doing and simply translate them to the digital world, we’re missing out on the opportunities technology gives us to completely transform the way things can be done. Transformation needs to focus on your customers, delivering new, personalized, connected experiences.

Watch the video

And it needs to center around your employees, digitizing business processes to increase their productivity and collaboration.

We might think of these are two distinct groups of people. But at the end of the day, the need to transform experiences and not just translate them, for both customers and employees, makes them two sides of the same coin.

1. Modern Application Lifecycle Management (ALM): The fast, efficient, and trusted path to building applications and has 8 tools for planning, coding, testing, and releasing robust apps.

  • CLI for integration with 3rd party editors
  • IDEs, text editors, language services
  • Builders (i.e.App Builder, Flow Builder, Schema Builder, Process Builder) and Wizards
  • Development environments (i.e.scratch orgs, developer and developer pro sandboxes
  • Continuous integration (CI) / Test Automation
  • Continuous delivery (CD) / Release Automation
  • Test environments (i.e.partial and full sandboxes)
  • Unlocked packages

2. It is easier to plan, code, build, test, and release apps that boost productivity without disrupting your business.

3. These capabilities allow you to leverage modern DevOps practices and standards, such as building together from your source control system, without any added complexity.

When you can deliver ideas to market faster, it’s easier to innovate and future-proof your business, and we deliver some of our most exciting innovations right out of the box for you...

ALM is a set of DevOps capabilities, and a repeatable process around them, designed to bring ideas to production fast, and in a safe way. We avoid unnecessary development in production, ensure the reliability of our critical workflows, but still free up all builders to try new things out and safely ferry those changes through a well-oiled test and release cycle. And it is a cycle - it’s never done. You need to internalize a process like this so that your business doesn’t have random ways of delivering apps, with varied dependencies and quirks that make owning them for years hard - because that’s the thing, you build this for the long-haul. This is also not a panacea, but it is a solution, and one designed around iteration - you can try this and own it, and it will reflect how your business works in time.
